About QR PhotoShare

QR PhotoShare was created to solve a simple problem: sharing high-quality photos with people around you without the hassle of exchanging phone numbers, emails, or dealing with compressed images on messaging apps.

Whether you are at a wedding, a concert, or just hanging out with friends, you can instantly create a secure session and let others download original photos with a single scan.
